# Golden Reaper - Pineapple, Lime & Carolina Reaper Mead (5 L, 12–13% ABV)

Tropical capsicumel made with pineapple, lime and one whole Carolina Reaper. This is deliberately an experimental, intensely chili-forward batch.

## Target Stats

* **Batch size:** 5.0 L
* **OG:** ~1.095–1.100
* **FG before backsweetening:** ~0.998–1.005
* **ABV:** ~12–13%
* **Final sweetness:** Adjust to taste after stabilization
* **Fermentation temperature:** 18–22°C
* **Chili level:** Intentionally very hot

---

## Fermentables & Fruit

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Honey – primary fermentation | **~1.25–1.30 kg** |
| Honey – reserved for backsweetening | **100–300 g** |
| Pineapple in juice | **125–150 g**, including juice |
| Lime | **¼ lime** |
| Carolina Reaper | **1 whole pepper (~10 g gross)** |
| Brown sugar | **12–15 g** |

### Important experimental choice

A strict 50% scale of the original 10 L recipe would use **½ Carolina Reaper (~5 g)**.

For this experimental batch, use the **whole ~10 g Reaper** if the purpose is specifically to find out what a genuinely Reaper-forward mead tastes like.

That gives approximately twice the Reaper concentration of the original recipe.

---

# Carolina Reaper, Pineapple & Lime Purée

## Preparing the Reaper

Wear disposable gloves.

Remove **only the stem**.

Keep:

* red flesh
* internal membranes / placenta
* seeds

After removing the stem, **weigh the usable pepper and record the weight**. This will make the experiment reproducible.

## Preparing the Lime

Use:

* juice from approximately **¼ lime**
* finely grated green zest from the same portion

Avoid the white pith.

## Blending

Blend together:

* **125–150 g pineapple and juice**
* the **whole prepared Carolina Reaper**
* **¼ lime juice**
* **¼ lime zest**

Blend thoroughly into a homogeneous purée.

## Pasteurisation

| Step | Temperature | Time |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Heat | → 72–75°C | — |
| Hold | **72–75°C** | **15 min** |
| Cool | → 18–22°C | — |

Heat gently and stir to keep the temperature reasonably uniform.

**Do not boil.**

Keep covered while cooling.

Once cooled substantially, stir in **12–15 g brown sugar** until dissolved.

### Safety

Blending and heating a whole Carolina Reaper can expose you to highly irritating capsaicin-containing droplets and vapour.

* Wear gloves.
* Ventilate the room well.
* Do not put your face over the blender when opening it.
* Do not lean over the saucepan.
* Avoid vigorous boiling or splashing.
* Thoroughly clean everything that contacted the pepper.

# Must Preparation

Start with approximately **3.0–3.3 L water**.

Warm enough of it to approximately **50–60°C** to dissolve the honey easily.

Dissolve approximately **1.25–1.30 kg honey**.

Do not boil the honey.

Add the entire cooled pineapple-lime-Reaper purée, **including all solids**.

Add cool water until approaching 5 L and mix extremely thoroughly.

Measure gravity.

**Target OG: 1.095–1.100.**

Use the measured OG rather than the calculated honey quantity as the final authority:

* **Below target:** add a little more honey.
* **Above ~1.105:** dilute with water.
* Once correct, adjust to approximately **5.0 L total volume**.

---

# Fermentation

Cool to **18–22°C**, transfer to a sanitised fermenter and aerate thoroughly.

Pitch **Mangrove Jack's M05 Mead Yeast**.

Because this contains pineapple solids, leave adequate headspace.

## Nutrient Schedule

Using DAP + ammonium-phosphate nutrient:

| Timing | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| At pitch | **2.0 g** |
| ~24 hours | **1.0 g** |
| ~48 hours | **1.0 g** |
| **Total** | **4.0 g** |

Dissolve each dose in a small amount of water or must before adding.

Degas carefully before nutrient additions because the fruit solids can encourage considerable foaming.

# Fermentation & Reaper Extraction

| Day | Stage | Approx. SG | Notes |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 0 | Pitch M05 | 1.095–1.100 | Entire purée added |
| 1 | Active fermentation | ~1.070–1.085 | Second nutrient addition |
| 2 | Strong fermentation | ~1.050–1.070 | Final nutrient addition |
| 3–5 | Main fermentation | Falling | Reaper heat should already be obvious |
| **7** | Main fermentation | Falling | **First useful chili assessment** |
| **10–14** | Slowing fermentation | ~1.005–1.020 | Taste again; substantial extraction should have occurred |
| 14–21+ | Finishing | ~0.998–1.005 | Continue monitoring heat and gravity |
| 21+ | Stable FG | ~0.998–1.005 | Proceed once gravity is stable |

Do **not filter the purée before fermentation**. The solids remain in the fermenter so extraction can continue as the alcohol concentration increases.

Because this experimental version contains approximately **twice the Reaper concentration** of the original recipe, tasting at day 7 is particularly important.

If the heat has reached the level you want and fermentation is essentially finished, there is no reason to maintain prolonged contact just to reach an arbitrary number of days.

# Packaging

| Stage | Timing | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirm fermentation finished | SG stable ≥3 days | Ensure fermentation is complete |
| Final chili assessment | Before cold crash | Evaluate heat before removing solids |
| Cold crash | 48–72 h at 2–4°C | Compact yeast, pineapple and pepper sediment |
| Rack | After cold crash | Leave all sediment/purée behind |
| Backsweeten | After racking | Add honey according to bench trial |
| K-meta | After backsweetening | **0.30 g** |
| Potassium sorbate | After K-meta | **0.8 g** |
| Stabilization rest | 24–48 h at 2–4°C | Keep sealed and cold |
| Stability check | After stabilization | Verify gravity remains stable |
| Bottle | Once stable | Bottle still |

# Backsweetening

This is particularly important for the experiment because sweetness will strongly affect your perception of the Reaper.

Perform bench trials with **100 mL** samples:

| Honey / 100 mL | Equivalent for 5 L |
| ---: | ---: |
| 2 g | 100 g |
| 3 g | 150 g |
| 4 g | 200 g |
| 5 g | 250 g |
| 6 g | 300 g |

Start around **2 g/100 mL** and work upward.

Don't taste the samples rapidly one after another. Carolina Reaper heat builds and persists, making subsequent samples difficult to compare accurately.

Scale the winning concentration according to the **actual volume recovered after racking**, not the original 5 L.

# Profile Notes

* **Aroma:** Honey, pineapple, fresh lime and fruity Carolina Reaper.
* **Flavour:** Tropical pineapple and honey followed by citrus and rapidly developing chili.
* **Chili:** **Extremely prominent by design.** One whole Reaper in only 5 L makes this substantially more aggressive than the original formulation.
* **Sweetness:** Determine experimentally after fermentation. Moderate sweetness should counterbalance some of the perceived heat and acidity.
* **Acidity:** Medium to moderately high from pineapple and lime.
* **Body:** Medium to medium-full after backsweetening.
* **Alcohol:** ~12–13% ABV.
* **Finish:** Long, tropical and intensely warming/hot.
* **Appearance:** Golden to amber; significant initial haze and sediment from the purée.
* **Overall character:** An intentionally extreme experimental capsicumel where Carolina Reaper is a defining ingredient rather than a background spice.
