# 🍺 Syrup Night - Stroopwafel Christmas Amber Ale (20L, 7%)

Braumeister All-Grain – Caramel-Dense Christmas Ale

## Target Stats

* **Batch Size**: 20 L
* **OG**: ~1.068–1.072
* **FG**: ~1.016–1.019
* **ABV**: ~6.7–7.2%
* **IBU**: ~19
* **EBC**: ~25–30 (deep caramel/red-amber)
* **Carbonation**: ~2.3–2.4 vol CO₂
* **Ready to drink**: ~6 weeks
* **Best drinking window**: ~8–12 weeks

---

## Grain Bill (Braumeister-friendly)

| Grain                    |      Amount |        % |
| ------------------------ | ----------: | -------: |
| Pale Ale Malt            |     4.20 kg |      70% |
| Munich Malt              |       700 g |      12% |
| CaraRed                  |       500 g |       8% |
| Biscuit Malt             |       300 g |       5% |
| Flaked Oats              |       300 g |       5% |
| **Total actual grain**   | **6.00 kg** | **100%** |
| Stroopwafels – mash      |   **350 g** |        — |
| Stroopwafels – late boil |   **250 g** |        — |
| Rice Hulls               |      ~200 g |        — |

The stroopwafels aren't counted in the grain percentages because their extract contribution varies by brand.

The grain bill is deliberately straightforward: **Munich for bread crust, Biscuit for toasted wafer, CaraRed for caramel and colour, and oats for a soft dessert-like texture**.

## Water

### Profile (recommended)

* **Ca**: 70–90 ppm
* **Mg**: 5–10 ppm
* **Cl**: 100–120 ppm
* **SO₄**: 45–60 ppm
* **Chloride:sulfate**: approximately 2:1

This is intentionally malt-forward and soft rather than crisp.

## Recipe

### Grain Distribution

| Ingredient      | Braumeister | Mini-Mash |       Total |
| --------------- | ----------: | --------: | ----------: |
| Pale Ale Malt   | **4.00 kg** | **200 g** | **4.20 kg** |
| Munich Malt     |       700 g |         — |       700 g |
| CaraRed         |       500 g |         — |       500 g |
| Biscuit Malt    |       300 g |         — |       300 g |
| Flaked Oats     |       300 g |         — |       300 g |
| **Total grain** | **5.80 kg** | **200 g** | **6.00 kg** |
| Stroopwafels    |           — | **350 g** |       350 g |
| Rice Hulls      |      ~200 g |         — |      ~200 g |

Notes:
- Mix the rice hulls throughout the grist rather than putting them in a separate layer.
- The remaining **250 g stroopwafels are reserved for the 5-minute boil addition** and are not part of either mash.

### Main Braumeister Mash

**LalBrew Windsor** is why we're now mashing at **67°C**, rather than the usual 68.5–69°C. Windsor should already leave substantial body and residual extract, so we don't need an extremely dextrinous mash as well.

| Minute | Step             | Target Temp (°C) | Action / Notes                          |
| -----: | ---------------- | ---------------: | --------------------------------------- |
|      0 | Dough-in         |           **67** | Add the 5.80 kg main grist + rice hulls |
|   0–60 | Saccharification |           **67** | Hold for 60 min                         |
|  60–70 | Mash-out ramp    |          67 → 75 | Raise gradually                         |
|  70–80 | Mash-out         |           **75** | Hold for 10 min                         |
|     80 | End mash         |                — | Lift malt pipe and drain                |
|      — | Sparge           |           **75** | Sparge to target pre-boil volume        |

### Stroopwafel Mini-Mash

Start this while the Braumeister is doing its main mash so it doesn't add much time to the brew day.

| Ingredient    |     Amount |
| ------------- | ---------: |
| Stroopwafels  |  **350 g** |
| Pale Ale Malt |  **200 g** |
| Water         | **~2.0 L** |

### Lauter / Sparge

* Lift the malt pipe after mash-out.
* Sparge gently with water at approximately **75°C**.
* Target approximately **25–26 L pre-boil**, adjusted for your established Braumeister losses.
* Allow plenty of time for drainage rather than compressing the grain bed.

### Hop Schedule (~19 IBU total)

| Minute | Main mash        | Mini-mash        | Addition                  | Notes                           |
| -----: | ---------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
|      0 | Start of boil    | -                | -                         | Start counting at 100°C         |
|     30 | 60 min remaining | -                | 10 g Magnum               | Bittering                       |
|     60 | -                | Start of boil    | -                         | Keep simmering if too fast      |
|     80 | 10 min remaining | -                | 20 g Goldings             | Subtle hop character            |
|     85 | -                | 5 min remaining  | **250 g stroopwafels**    | Late flavour addition           |
|     85 | -                | 5 min remaining  | **0.5 g ground cinnamon** | Reinforces stroopwafel spice    |
|     85 | -                | 5 min remaining  | **0.2 g ground nutmeg**   | Background bakery warmth        |
|     85 | -                | 5 min remaining  | **3 g orange zest**       | Brightness                      |
|     90 | Flameout         | Flameout         | —                         | -                               |
|     90 | Post-Flameout    | -                | Add mini-mash to mash     | Rinse pot with hot wort if necessary |

### Mini-Mash + Main Braumeister Timeline

| Braumeister – Main Batch                                                                                  | Stroopwafel Mini-Mash / Concentrate                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Main mash – 67°C, 60 min**                                                                              | **Start mini-mash:** Heat ~2 L water and stabilize at **65–66°C**. Add **200 g Pale Ale Malt + 350 g broken stroopwafels**.                                                     |
| Continue main mash at **67°C**                                                                            | Hold mini-mash at **65–66°C for 40 min**. Stir gently every 5–10 min.                                                                                                           |
| Raise to **75°C** for mash-out                                                                            | After 40 min, strain mini-mash through a brew bag. Allow to drain; gently squeeze. Collect the cloudy stroopwafel wort.                                                         |
| Hold mash-out at **75°C for 10 min**                                                                      | Cover the strained concentrate and set aside.                                                                                                                                   |
| Lift malt pipe and sparge with **~75°C water**                                                            | Keep concentrate covered. No heating required yet.                                                                                                                              |
| Bring main wort toward boil                                                                               | Keep concentrate covered.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Start 90 min boil**                                                                                     | Concentrate remains covered and set aside.                                                                                                                                      |
| **60 min remaining:** Add **11 g Magnum** *(assuming ~13.6% AA; adjust to actual packet AA if different)* | Concentrate remains covered.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **30 min remaining:** Continue normal boil                                                                | Begin gently reheating the stroopwafel concentrate. Keep covered initially and stir periodically.                                                                               |
| **~20–15 min remaining:** Continue normal boil                                                            | Once concentrate reaches a boil, reduce to a very gentle simmer. If it reaches boiling early, heat may be switched off and the pot kept covered. Avoid unnecessary evaporation. |
| **10 min remaining:** Add **20 g Goldings**                                                               | Ensure concentrate is hot. If heat was switched off, begin bringing it back to a boil now.                                                                                      |
| **7–8 min remaining:** Continue main boil                                                                 | Concentrate should now be approaching or at a gentle boil. Stir to prevent settling/scorching.                                                                                  |
| **5 min remaining:** Continue main boil                                                                   | **Add 250 g broken stroopwafels + 0.5 g ground cassia cinnamon + 0.15 g ground nutmeg + 3 g candied orange peel.** Maintain a gentle boil for **5 min**, stirring frequently.   |
| **0 min – Flameout:** Switch off Braumeister heater                                                       | After exactly ~5 min with the late additions, switch off the pot heat.                                                                                                          |
| **Immediately post-flameout:** Main wort remains hot; do not begin chilling yet                           | Pour the **entire hot stroopwafel concentrate** into the Braumeister.                                                                                                           |
| **Post-flameout:** Stir/whirlpool thoroughly to distribute the concentrate through the main wort          | Rinse the small pot with a small amount of hot wort if needed to recover remaining caramel/concentrate; return the rinse to the Braumeister.                                    |
| **After concentrate is fully incorporated:** Begin chilling to **18°C**                                   | Mini-mash/concentrate process complete.                                                                           

### Cooling

* Chill promptly to **18°C**.
* Transfer to fermenter.
* Aerate thoroughly.

### Fermentation

Use a **moderately attenuative English ale yeast**.

**LalBrew Windsor** would fit the concept well: malt-forward with restrained English fruit character and lower attenuation than many Belgian or American strains.

We will need 2 bags, according to the [Pitch Rate Calculator](https://www.brewersfriend.com/yeast-pitch-rate-and-starter-calculator/).

### Fermentation Timeline

|    Day | Stage               | Temperature | Notes                                  |
| -----: | ------------------- | ----------: | -------------------------------------- |
|      0 | Pitch               |        18°C | Aerate wort thoroughly                 |
|    1–2 | Main fermentation   |        18°C | Hold temperature                       |
|    3–4 | Active fermentation |        19°C | Allow gentle rise                      |
|    5–7 | Finishing           |        20°C | Let fermentation finish naturally      |
|   8–10 | Cleanup             |     20–21°C | Keep on yeast                          |
|    ~10 | First gravity check |     20–21°C | Don't package based on target FG alone |
| ~12–14 | Stable FG           |     20–21°C | Package only after gravity is stable   |

Expected FG is approximately **1.016–1.019**, but the actual stable FG determines when fermentation is complete.

### Dry-Hopping Schedule

| Day | Expected SG | Stage | Temperature | Hop Addition | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---| --- |
| ~9–10 | Near FG | Vanilla conditioning | 20°C | **½ vanilla bean** | Taste after ~3 days |
| ~12–14 | Stable | Remove/package | — | — | Don't let vanilla dominate |

Split and scrape approximately **½ vanilla bean** before adding it.

The vanilla isn't intended to make this taste like vanilla beer. Its job is to reinforce the perception of **sweet caramel and pastry**.

Taste after approximately 3 days. If the vanilla is already clearly identifiable, remove/package rather than automatically leaving it for 5 days.

No dry hops.

### Packaging

* Target **~2.3–2.4 vol CO₂**.
* For bottles, allow approximately **2 weeks around 20°C** for carbonation.
* Then condition cooler.
* Don't attempt to retain sweetness by packaging before fermentation is complete.

### Conditioning Time – Overview

| Method / Time        | Conditioning Time | Notes                                       |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Primary fermentation | ~2 weeks          | Reach stable FG                             |
| Bottle carbonation   | ~2 weeks          | ~20°C                                       |
| Early conditioning   | Weeks 4–6         | Spice/caramel begins integrating            |
| **Drinkable**        | **~6 weeks**      | Still relatively young                      |
| **Recommended**      | **8–10 weeks**    | ⭐ Best balance of waffle, caramel and spice |
| Fully conditioned    | ~12 weeks         | Comfortably inside 3-month target           |

## Final Notes

The intended flavour structure is:

**Caramel → toasted stroopwafel → biscuit → bread crust → creamy malt → cinnamon → subtle vanilla/nutmeg → light orange**

The spices should support the stroopwafels rather than turn this into a generic Christmas-spice beer.

I would **not add lactose, maltodextrin, malt extract or Syrup Malt**. The current grain bill already gives us several complementary mechanisms:

* **500 g CaraRed** → caramel/toffee + red colour
* **700 g Munich** → rich malt/bread crust
* **300 g Biscuit** → toasted wafer
* **300 g oats** → fullness and softness
* **68.5°C mash** → more dextrinous wort
* **600 g stroopwafels** → actual caramel/wafer/cinnamon character
* **~19 IBU** → enough balance without suppressing perceived sweetness

The main unknown is exactly how much flavour, extract and fat your particular stroopwafels contribute. For the first brew, I would therefore **not chase the predicted OG by adding sugar**. Record the actual OG and FG and use those results to tune batch two.
